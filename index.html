School Identity & Profile

About Waiheke High

Est. 1986 Years 7–13

Established in 1986, Waiheke High School is the only island based high school in New Zealand and caters for students from years 7-13.

In a relatively short time, we have accomplished an enviable academic record, recognising the top overall scholar and top Māori student of New Zealand as just some of our students’ outstanding successes.

Located in the pristine environment of the Hauraki Gulf / Tīkapa Moana, Waiheke High School leverages its unique island setting to provide world-class Education Outside the Classroom (EOTC). Our outdoor programmes build resilience, environmental kaitiakitanga, and leadership skills across every year level.

📋 Camp Summary

*Activities may change depending on staffing and weather.
DAY 1: Ferry Transit & Camp Setup Matiatia to Motuihe

DOC quarantine inspection at Matiatia Wharf. Ferry to Motuihe Island. Establish campsite, pitch gazebos and tents, set up cooking area and water systems.

DAY 1: Mau Rākau & Cultural Heritage Pā Horo & History

Mau Rākau training sessions covering stances, karakia, waiata, and the history of Motuihe Pā. Evening team sports (cricket, vortex, touch rugby).

DAY 2: Teambuilding, Fishing & Beach Art Wharf & Ocean Beach

Teambuilding rotation (ropes, barrels, blindfold maze). Coastal fishing off wharf/rocky points, followed by creative beach art using natural island materials.

DAY 2: DOC Ecosystem Conservation Native Nursery & Planting

Voluntary conservation service project with Motuihe Trust: potting native seedlings, nursery tasks, and predator-free biosecurity awareness.

DAY 3: Biosecurity Clean-Down & Return Departure to Waiheke

Campsite pack-down, biosecurity gear inspection, BBQ lunch, reflection circle, and ferry return to Waiheke Island.

Motuihe Sanctuary Tracks

Motuihe Headland Circuit 4.5 km / 2 hrs

Panoramic coastal cliffs overlooking Waitematā Harbour. Open pasture and steep drops requiring stay-on-path discipline.

Tīeke Bush Reserve Path 2.0 km / 45 mins

Regenerating forest canopy containing tīeke (saddleback) and native fauna burrows.

🌿 Strict DOC Sanctuary Biosecurity

Motuihe Island is a pest-free reserve. All backpacks must be vacuumed, velcro cleared of seeds, and all food sealed in hard plastic containers prior to departure.
